This is massively underestimated.

You need to be able to defend your projects.

For every project:

```
Why did you build it?
Why this architecture?
Why PostgreSQL?
Why Redis?
Why Kafka?
What happens if Kafka dies?
What happens if DB dies?
How does authentication work?
How would you scale it?
What was the hardest bug?
What would you change?
What tradeoff did you make?
```

Recent interview reports also describe deep questioning around candidates' projects and design decisions.
